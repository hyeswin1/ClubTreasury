Core Safety Invariants & Anti-Abuse Protections

How this dApp guarantees transparency and accountability to judges and members.

✓ Who is allowed to do what?

Anyone can deposit funds into the treasury vault. Only verified club representatives can submit proposals and cast votes. Once a proposal passes quorum and deadline, any member of the public can trigger execution.

✓ How decisions about money get made?

Funds can only be disbursed through an on-chain proposal containing the title, estimated expense, and direct vendor address. A strict voting threshold (>50% Yes and ≥30% Quorum) must be satisfied.

✓ Why no single person can quietly misuse funds?

Zero backdoors: The smart contract contains no owner withdrawal function, no emergency drain, and no upgradeable proxy backdoor. Funds are sent straight to the vendor recipient address specified in the voted proposal.

✓ What a member can verify publicly afterwards?

Every incoming donation, proposal vote, and vendor payment emits permanent on-chain events and is human-auditable in the Transaction Log page.
